BeUnbridled Setup Guide

Create Your Agent's Telegram Bot

It takes about 5 minutes. You only do it once.

1

Download Telegram

Visit telegram.org or your device's App Store/Google Play to download the app. Create an account, then open Settings → Edit Profile → Username to set a public username.

2

Open BotFather

In Telegram, search for @BotFather (look for the blue checkmark) and tap on it. Then, tap the "Start" button to begin interacting with the official Telegram bot creator.

3

Create your bot

Send the command /newbot to BotFather. Follow the prompts:

  • Pick a display name for your bot (e.g., "BeUnbridled Agent").
  • Pick a username for your bot. This must be unique and must end in 'bot' (e.g., "MyUnbridledAssistantBot"). Once set, this username is permanent.

BotFather will then give you an HTTP API token, which looks something like 8673964185:ABCDEFG12345HIJKLMN67890.

4

Send us your token

Copy the full API token provided by BotFather. Send it to your BeUnbridled onboarding contact or reply to your onboarding email with the token. Treat this token like a password and keep it safe.

⚠️ Keep your bot token private. Don't share it publicly or commit it to code.

That's it. We'll take it from here and have your agent live within 24 hours.
