06 — Voice & Tone

How We Sound

Precision meets warmth. We're not a cold corporate AI tool — we're human people who built something precise and reliable, and we talk like it.

Do
  • Short, declarative sentences
  • Plain English, no jargon
  • Specifics: "24 hours" not "quickly"
  • First-person "we" — you're in this
  • Commas, not em-dashes
  • Warmth through specificity
Don't
  • "Seamless"
  • "Leverage" or "Utilize"
  • "World-class" / "Best-in-class"
  • "Cutting-edge AI-powered"
  • "Unlock your potential"
  • Em-dash-heavy prose
