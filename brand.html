06 — Voice & Tone

How We Sound

Inherited from Unbridled DNA — adapted for B2B SaaS. Precise but human. Confident, not arrogant. Founder-facing, not IT-facing.

Do
  • Short, declarative sentences
  • Plain English over jargon
  • Specificity: "24 hours" not "quickly"
  • Honest claims only
  • Commas, not em-dashes
  • Action-forward: every line earns its keep
Don't
  • "Seamless"
  • "Leverage" / "Utilize"
  • "World-class" / "Best-in-class"
  • "Cutting-edge" / "AI-powered" (generic)
  • "Unlock your potential"
  • Em-dash-heavy prose
